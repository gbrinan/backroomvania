Environment-only background for BACKROOMVANIA, a mature dark fantasy Victorian museum Backrooms world. No people, no monsters, no human silhouettes, no text, no UI. Endless liminal architectural repetition, abandoned utility space, oppressive silence, dark blue-green and muted amber palette. Most important: water acts like a mirror into an impossible second architecture, repeated mirror reflections and their reflections recede endlessly, stretched vertical reflections softly waver with tiny ripples. Clear near detail gradually dissolves into distant haze. Rich carefully clustered pixel-art inspired game environment, not photoreal photography. Low camera just above perfectly still black water. Vast long narrow flooded museum service corridor, stained pale yellow walls, identical rectangular doorframes and dark brass pipes receding to a single distant vanishing point. Tall oxidized mirrors set in doorways face one another, creating nested corridors. Upper architecture and reflection meet at a thin waterline near center; underwater reflected arches extend MUCH deeper than the shallow water could allow. Few amber gaslamps become infinite stretched ribbons in the water. No terminating wall or visible end. Restrained composition, empty and unsettling, water occupies lower half.
