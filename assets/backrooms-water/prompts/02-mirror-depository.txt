Environment-only background for BACKROOMVANIA, a mature dark fantasy Victorian museum Backrooms world. No people, no monsters, no human silhouettes, no text, no UI. Endless liminal architectural repetition, abandoned utility space, oppressive silence, dark blue-green and muted amber palette. Most important: water acts like a mirror into an impossible second architecture, repeated mirror reflections and their reflections recede endlessly, stretched vertical reflections softly waver with tiny ripples. Clear near detail gradually dissolves into distant haze. Rich carefully clustered pixel-art inspired game environment, not photoreal photography. Wide eye-level view across a flooded archive warehouse. Countless tall antique rectangular mirrors with restrained tarnished brass frames stand in precisely repeated parallel rows on submerged stone plinths. Frame within frame within frame, reflections repeat sideways and below the water to form an endless grid of rooms. Water is ink-black with pale teal reflective edges; occasional mirrors reflect distant ceiling lamps that do not exist overhead. Vast low ceiling with repetitive square pillars, no palace luxury. Closest central mirror shows another identical flooded room, then another, no horizon end. Mirror recursion is the main subject, not broken glass.
